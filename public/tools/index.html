◈ YOUR ESTIMATE

Zakat calculator

Enter nisab from a trusted local source, then estimate your zakatable assets.

Nisab is commonly estimated as the value of 85 g of gold or 595 g of silver. Choose a basis and enter its current local price per gram, or enter the threshold manually. No live metal price is assumed.

How is this estimate calculated?

Net zakatable assets = cash and bank balances + zakatable metals/trade goods + eligible receivables − debts due soon (not below zero). If this reaches the selected nisab and the applicable lunar-year conditions are met, the default estimate is 2.5% of net assets. You can change the rate above. This is a calculator, not a fatwa; asset and debt rulings may vary by school and circumstance.
